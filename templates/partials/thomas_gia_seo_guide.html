Thomas GIA preparation guide

Prepare for all five Thomas GIA sections

The Thomas General Intelligence Assessment is a speed-focused cognitive ability test. Familiarity with the instructions and question styles helps you spend less time interpreting the task and more time answering accurately.

What free practice includes

Try representative practice questions, receive immediate feedback in practice mode, and become familiar with the reasoning, perception, numerical, verbal, and spatial tasks used across the five sections.

How to practise effectively

Learn one format at a time before adding time pressure. Review incorrect answers, identify your slowest section, and repeat short drills while keeping accuracy steady rather than guessing faster.

Practice under realistic pressure

Once the formats feel familiar, use timed module tests and the guided full-test flow to practise switching between sections and maintaining concentration.

Free Thomas GIA practice questions: common questions

Is the Thomas GIA practice test free?

MindMetric provides free practice access so you can try the section formats without entering card details. Additional attempts or features may depend on the access plan shown on the site.

Which Thomas GIA sections can I practise?

Practice covers Reasoning, Perceptual Speed, Number Speed and Accuracy, Word Meaning, and Spatial Visualisation. Each module focuses on its own question format and pace.

Are these official Thomas International questions?

No. MindMetric uses independently created practice material designed to build familiarity with comparable skills and formats. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Thomas International.

What is the best way to prepare for the GIA test?

First understand every section's instructions, then practise for accuracy, and finally add timed repetition. Tracking both pace and mistakes gives you a more useful preparation target than speed alone.
